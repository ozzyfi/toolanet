Privacy & data use

This notice explains how ToolA processes personal data you provide through the pilot meeting form.

1. Data controller

ToolA Teknoloji A.Ş. is the data controller for the personal data collected through this form. You can contact us at info@toola.net for privacy and personal data requests.

2. What data do we collect?

The pilot meeting form collects your full name, company, phone number and email address. The phone field is optional. We do not request technical system, equipment or operational data through this form.

3. Why do we use the data?

We use the data to receive and respond to your pilot meeting request, contact you, schedule the meeting, route your request to the relevant ToolA team and maintain the security of the communication process. We do not use the information submitted through this form for bulk marketing communications unless you separately opt in.

4. Processing method and legal basis

The data is provided by you electronically through this form. Processing is carried out to respond to your request, take steps toward a potential business relationship and pursue ToolA’s legitimate interests in managing communications and business development, provided that your fundamental rights and freedoms are not overridden.

5. Who may receive the data?

Form submissions are processed through Formspree, our form infrastructure provider, and may be viewed by authorized ToolA personnel. Due to the technical operation of the service, your data may be processed outside Türkiye. International transfers are assessed separately under applicable data protection law.

6. How long do we keep it?

We keep the data for as long as needed to handle the pilot meeting and related communication, and afterwards only for the period required by applicable law, potential legal claims and legitimate record-keeping obligations. Data that is no longer needed is deleted, destroyed or anonymized.

7. Your rights

You may contact ToolA at info@toola.net to exercise your rights under applicable personal data protection law, including the rights provided under Article 11 of Türkiye’s Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698. Requests are handled subject to identity verification and applicable law.

8. About Formspree

Formspree is the third-party form infrastructure used to transmit this form. Formspree processes form submissions under its own security and privacy practices, and its own privacy terms also apply.

This notice applies only to the website pilot meeting form. Separate contractual and data-processing arrangements may apply to technical or operational data processed by the ToolA product in a customer environment.
